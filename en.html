My Water Privacy Policy

Dated 5 November 2026 · Android · com.dalexsoft.watertracker

My Water (Моя вода, WaterTracker) is developed by an individual based in Russia using the public name DalexSoft. For privacy and data processing enquiries, contact DalexSoftCustomer@outlook.com.

1 App data on your device

My Water stores the gender, weight and activity level you enter, your calculated or manually selected daily goal, drink entries with their volumes and times, and custom drinks and their properties. It also keeps daily goal history, reminder, vibration and quick-add settings, your selected language and initial setup status on your device. Statistics are calculated from this information.

Your weight, activity information and drink diary may be personal information relating to health and lifestyle. The app uses this information to calculate your daily goal, record drinks and display statistics. It does not send this information to the developer or store it on the developer’s server. No account or registration is required.

To operate reminders, the app stores the next scheduled time and selected interval. Reminder messages may show the amount remaining towards your daily goal. Notifications may appear on your lock screen and in Android notification history, depending on your device settings.

The app stores the identifier of the policy revision you confirm on your device. This record controls the acknowledgement dialog at startup and is not sent to the developer. You can open the policy from that dialog or from the app’s settings.

The current version disables Android backup and system device-to-device transfer of the app’s data. The developer does not provide cloud synchronisation or backup storage. This does not remove copies that may have been created by earlier versions or saved by you separately; those copies are managed independently.

2 Analytics advertising and permissions

The app does not include analytics services, advertising SDKs or automatic crash reporting. It does not display advertisements. It does not send usage events, diary entries, advertising identifiers or technical statistics to the developer. The app does not request Internet access permission.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, messages, photos or documents. It does not connect to Health Connect or automatically obtain information from health sensors. You enter the profile and diary information yourself.

Reminders use Android permission to display notifications and, on supported Android versions, special access to schedule exact alarms. Without notification permission, messages are not displayed. Without exact alarm access, reminders continue with approximate timing and may be delayed. You can change these permissions in Android settings.

System facilities for background work, briefly waking the device, vibration and restoring the schedule after a restart are used to deliver reminders. The app does not use them to send your information to the developer. Android controls notification delivery and visibility.

3 Who may receive information

The app does not transmit your local profile, drink diary or settings to the developer, advertising partners or analytics providers. The developer does not sell user data.

If you choose to contact support, the developer receives your sender address, message and any materials you attach. Email delivery and storage also involve email service providers under their terms. These services may process correspondence outside your country.

When you open the policy, the app passes the page address to your browser. Your browser and the hosting service load the page; section 8 explains how page visits are handled. Android, your app store and your email application are also governed by their respective providers’ policies.

4 Security and retention

App data is kept in local app storage, with access restricted by Android. The app does not provide separate database encryption or a PIN-based entry lock. The protection of your device depends on your Android security settings.

Your profile, diary, drink information, goal history and settings remain until changed or deleted, or until app storage is cleared. Deleting a custom drink removes it from the active list, but its record may remain in the database, including for links to historical entries. Turning reminders off cancels the schedule and background check; it does not erase your diary or profile.

Support correspondence is used to respond and handle your request. Once the enquiry is resolved, it is retained only where needed for legal obligations or dispute resolution. The email service’s own terms govern its retention and deletion of messages.

5 Deletion and enquiries

You can edit your profile and delete individual drink entries in the app. To remove all local app data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Normal updates to an installed copy of the app preserve its data.

In the current version, the “Reset all data” action resets your profile and shows the initial setup screens again. It does not delete drink history, custom drinks, daily goal history, reminder settings or the stored policy revision. For complete removal, use the Android controls described above.

System notification history, previously created backups and copies of materials you have sent to other recipients are managed separately. Clearing app storage does not delete emails you have sent to support.

For enquiries about processing, correction or deletion of information in support correspondence, email DalexSoftCustomer@outlook.com and identify My Water and your request. The developer cannot remotely view or erase information stored only on your device. Please do not send health information or other personal materials unless they are needed to resolve your enquiry.

6 Children’s data and user rights

The app does not request a date of birth or verify age. Information a child enters locally is not automatically sent to the developer either. If a child’s personal information has been sent to support, a parent or legal guardian may send a deletion request to the contact address above.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict processing or object to it. Requests can be sent to the contact email and are handled within the procedures and deadlines set by applicable law. Information stored only on your device is managed on that device.

7 Processing purposes and policy acknowledgement

Local storage of your profile, diary and settings is needed for the features you select: goal calculation, drink tracking, statistics and reminders. Information in support enquiries is used to communicate with you, handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Selecting “I accept” acknowledges that you have read this policy. It does not remove your legal rights or replace separate consent where such consent is required. If new features require separate consent, it must be obtained before the relevant processing begins.

8 Policy changes and viewing this page

When data processing changes, the developer updates the policy text and date. The current revision is published on the policy page linked from the app. When the policy revision specified in the app is updated, the app requests acknowledgement again before you continue.

The policy opens in an external browser. The page does not use forms, analytics or advertising scripts, or externally hosted fonts. When you view the page on GitHub Pages, the hosting service records your IP address for security purposes under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Page visits are processed separately from the app’s local diary.
